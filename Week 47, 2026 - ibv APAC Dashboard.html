Summary

  • In Malaysia, the first CRESS solar project started construction while another 1,000 MWac CRESS MoU was signed. Corporate demand is clearly there to sell into, but almost all CRESS capacity is still on paper with a 31 December 2028 COD deadline, so the scarce inputs are grid, land and EPC capacity, and tie-ups like Samaiden's exclusive EPCC role are already locking some of that up.
  • In Indonesia, PLN signed six PPAs at COP31 and is readying the 1.9 GWp Mentari Nusantara 2 tender, its first real procurement momentum since the 100 GWp launch. This opens bundled IPP tenders and small island solar-plus-battery PPAs. On the other side, PLN subholdings take up to 30% of projects, partner slots with PLN Indonesia Power are going to large Chinese and regional players, and tender volumes are still a fraction of the programme target.
  • In the Philippines, ACEN gave back about 4 GW of early-stage Luzon contracts, including 2.3 GW of offshore wind, to focus on its Zambales solar and battery hub. It does not change Mindanao competition for GEA-7, but it is a read on ACEN's priorities, leaves one fewer large name in January's 3,300 MW offshore wind auction, and shows the DOE will look harder at contracts held to flip.

Topic 1 · From Malaysia (Nov 17, Nov 20)

Malaysia's first CRESS solar project is finally in the ground, while signed CRESS capacity keeps running well ahead of construction

On 20 November construction began on HLGY Energy's 120.06 MWac solar plant with a 240.12 MWh battery at Kedah Rubber City, the first CRESS solar project to break ground. Three days earlier Samaiden and Parkland Renewable Energy (a Parkland and Xinyi Energy JV) signed a one-year MoU for up to 1,000 MWac of CRESS projects across six states, with Samaiden as exclusive EPCC contractor and project sizes set by demand and grid availability. Set against the CRESS deals logged in the radar since mid-2025, the gap is clear: most are contracts, term sheets or MoUs, and on 14 October HLGY's Kedah plant was the only CRESS project in advanced construction. The clock matters because the September SAC package, now 18 sen/kWh including a 4 sen surcharge to end-2027, carries a hard COD deadline of 31 December 2028.

Topic 2 · From Indonesia (Nov 18, Nov 19, Nov 21)

PLN moves from announcing the 100 GWp programme to signing and tendering

In one week PLN signed six PPAs and two letters of intent at COP31, covering about 324 MW of solar and wind with batteries plus hydro. It said Mentari Nusantara 2, a 1.9 GWp bundled solar tender, is ready to launch after market consultations, and it consulted the market on the 1.2 GWac Bayu Nusantara wind project. It also secured AIIB financing for the 100 MW Gajahmungkur floating solar plant and broke ground on a 4.5 MW / 9 MWh hybrid on Nusa Penida. The contrast with April is sharp: the IEEFA entry then noted that no PPAs had been signed since 2022 despite Presidential Regulation 112/2022. The way PLN procures is also settling into a pattern: bundled multi-site tenders, PLN subholdings taking up to 30%, and PLN Indonesia Power Renewables pairing with private partners under the Proyek Hijaunesia staple financing (GCL at Gajahmungkur, Vena at Pasuruan). Scale is still the open question. Mentari Nusantara 2 is about 2 GWp against a programme pace of roughly 33 GWp a year.

Topic 3 · From the Philippines (Nov 19, Nov 20)

ACEN hands back about 4 GW of early-stage Luzon contracts, including both its offshore wind projects, weeks before the January offshore wind auction

On 19 November the DOE backed ACEN's voluntary surrender of nearly 5 GW of solar and wind service contracts, saying ACEN dropped projects it judged unviable instead of selling them, and that it does not want developers holding contracts simply to sell them on. The named projects add up to 4,015 MW and are all in Luzon, so they have no bearing on Mindanao-focused rounds such as GEA-7. What they do show is where ACEN is putting its money: away from early-stage offshore wind and large greenfield sites, and toward its execution-ready Zambales solar and battery hub. The timing stands out. A day later the DOE confirmed a 3,300 MW offshore wind auction for January 2027, and ACEN's 2,272 MW of offshore wind contracts will not be in the field.

DOE to Auction 3,000 MW of Natural Gas in December and 3,300 MW of Offshore Wind in January 2027; Further Auctions Planned for Floating Solar, Rooftop Solar, Biomass, Hydro and Pumped Storage

Energy Secretary Sharon Garin said on 20 November that the DOE will run a 3,000 MW natural gas auction in December and a 3,300 MW offshore wind auction in January 2027, 6,300 MW combined. The DOE has awarded about 20.7 GW of renewables across four Green Energy Auction rounds and plans another 25 GW for delivery between 2027 and 2035, against Philippine Energy Plan targets of 35% renewable generation by 2030 and 50% by 2040. Later auctions will cover floating solar, rooftop solar, off-grid systems, biomass, hydro and pumped storage. The first waste-to-energy auction identified nine potential winning bidders for 12 projects totalling 428 MW.

The Court of Appeals froze 41 bank accounts, five securities accounts and eight properties linked to Batangas Rep. Leandro Leviste, granting an Anti-Money Laundering Council petition. Korea Fair Trade Commission Raids Hanwha Solutions, HD Hyundai Energy Solutions and Hansol Technics Over Suspected Solar Panel Price Collusion

The KFTC carried out on-site inspections of Hanwha Solutions, HD Hyundai Energy Solutions and Hansol Technics on 20 November over suspected collusion on solar panel prices. The probe comes as Korean makers lose ground to cheaper Chinese products: Korean-made modules fell from 66% of the domestic market in 2021 to 30.7% last year, Korean-made cells from 35.1% to 3.9%, and utilisation at Hanwha Solutions' Korean module plants from 33% in 2024 to 20% last year. Business groups warn the probe could further chill a shrinking industry.
